Privacy Policy / プライバシーポリシー

Last updated: June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the application “SNS自動投稿 / Social Media Scheduler” (the “App”) handles information. The App is a private tool used by its operator to schedule and publish videos to social media accounts that the operator owns or is authorized to manage, including YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram.

本ポリシーは、アプリ「SNS自動投稿」が情報をどのように取り扱うかを説明するものです。 本アプリは、運営者が所有または管理を許可されたSNSアカウント(YouTube・TikTok・Instagram)へ 動画を予約投稿するための非公開ツールです。

1. Information We Access

With your explicit authorization through each platform’s official OAuth login, the App may access:

  • Basic account/profile information (such as account name and ID) needed to identify the connected account.
  • Permission to upload and post video content to the connected account on your behalf.
  • Video files that you choose to upload, which you provide via your own Google Drive folder.

The App does not collect personal information from any third parties and is not intended for use by the general public.

2. How Information Is Used

Accessed information is used solely to:

  • Authenticate the connected social media account.
  • Upload, schedule, and publish the videos and captions that you provide.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to publishing your own content.

3. Storage and Security

Access tokens issued by the platforms are stored locally and used only to perform the posting actions you request. They are not sold or shared with third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share accessed data with third parties. Content is transmitted only to the social media platform you instruct the App to post to (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram), through their official APIs.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You may revoke the App’s access at any time from the connected platform’s settings (e.g., your TikTok, Google, or Instagram account security settings). Upon revocation or upon your request, the corresponding stored access tokens are deleted. To request deletion, contact us at the address below.

6. Third-Party Platforms

The App interacts with YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram via their official APIs. Your use of those platforms is governed by their respective terms and privacy policies.

7. Contact
